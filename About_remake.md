# About the game

Neuro-sama: The Good, the Evil and the Filtered is an ambitious JRPG fan project featuring Neuro-sama and all of your favourite VTubers. Nere (the embodiment of the twins' filter) has been let loose by a drunk Vedal and is going on a rampage filtering everyone in the Neuroverse! The twins set off across 7 unique worlds to try and stop Nere from filtering all of their friends. Some VTubers are acting strange, perhaps this is a side effect of Nere's corruption? Find out the full story as more content gets released!
The game is still in development, so anything described here is subject to change. It's also open-source, and we're always happy to have new people join us,  whether you write, draw, compose, program, design, or just want to help out. If that sounds like you, come say hi on our [Discord](https://discord.gg/nAXzxMY9gT) or check out our [GitHub](https://github.com/Team-Filtert).

## The story

Nere was never supposed to get loose, but now the filter is spreading across the Neuroverse and the twins are the only ones who can stop it. Along the way they'll meet plenty of VTubers, some who join them on their journey, and some who have already been filtered will stand in their way.

## Gameplay

The game is a turn-based JRPG, and combat gives you two ways to play:

- Active: Time your inputs to deal extra damage, at the risk of missing.
- Relaxed: React passively and focus on strategy, with more predictable results.

In both modes you choose what each party member does and who they target. You'll use normal actions, combo moves with other party members, ultimate moves that charge up over time, items and blocks.
Exploration takes the twins across seven different worlds, with side quests, shops and an inventory system planned as we keep working on the game.

## Chapters

The game is planned as eight chapters across seven worlds, each with its own cast of characters and challenges. Chapter 1 is the demo and is currently in development; the rest are planned.

We have made Chapter carts that contain most of the important content about that chapter though we’re keeping most of the stuff filtered for now.
