## We're sorry
We are all pretty busy with life and couldn't work on the project as much as we wanted

But that doesn't mean we are giving up, we are still working hard on the project and want to get it done

Our current goal is to release a trailer to Neuro's birthday


## The good news
We still managed to get some things done, here is a small list of what we did:

### We made our own font
We made our own font based on the font used for Neuro's subtitles.
The original font didn't have all the characters we needed, so we added the missing ones to the font.

If you're interested in the font, you can find it <a href="/other/First_Coffee_Extended.otf" download>here</a> or on the game's github.
